# S12.0 — Wind/handling-noise baseline and AGC popping investigation

## Why we start read-only

S7–S11 have been accepted and merged. User hears intermittent popping and hiss when moving the microphone, and stage-bypass comparisons suggest S10 may contribute. Mechanical contact noise, airflow hitting the capsule, and VAD-triggered AGC amplification remain distinct plausible causes. **Do not alter S10 gains or mute the rider while wind is present without evidence.**

S12.0 is a **diagnostic-only** first step toward dedicated wind suppression. No filters, coefficients, VAD state, AGC gains, or PCM samples are modified by S12. This is NOT a tested wind classifier or wind-reduction feature yet.

## Read-only measurements

New `slipstream_s12.c` reads the 480 raw 48 kHz left/mic samples per 10ms block **before S7's 100 Hz high-pass**. It tracks:
- RMS of raw microphone signal.
- Approximate 20–200 Hz rumble band from two stateful one-pole lowpass filters (20 Hz, 200 Hz) and their difference.
- Ratio of wind-band RMS to raw RMS (percent, clipped to 100).
- Provisional wind candidate if band RMS >=250 PCM units and ratio >=60%. **Not calibrated; voice and mic handling can also trigger it.** No action is taken on this flag.
- Maximum 10ms S10 gain step in tenths dB, correlating pops/hiss with sudden AGC changes.
- Per-second count of candidate frames (`WBlocks`); usually 0–100 since ~99 blocks/s.

Output fields, appended to existing S11 nonblocking UART summary:
- `WRMS` = most recent block raw microphone RMS in PCM units
- `WBand` = most recent block approximate 20–200 Hz band RMS
- `WRatio` = most recent block band/raw RMS percentage, 0–100
- `WMax` = max band/raw RMS percentage across the report interval
- `WBlocks` = number of candidate frames across report interval
- `GStep10` = max absolute AGC gain change *10 (dB), per block, across report interval

The low-pass filters are **measurement-only**, not part of playback. Compare with existing `Speech`, `Start/End`, `Gain10`, `AClip`, `ClipD/F/NS/O`, `Late`, `Miss`, `DMA`, and `Proc`. Nonblocking USART3 buffer increased to 768 bytes to avoid truncation.

## Controlled tests — take one continuous UART log

Keep listening volume low during mechanical/wind disturbances, and begin with 1 sec of quiet startup for S8/S9 noise learning.

1. Quiet, mic stationary and untouched, 10 s.
2. Move cable/microphone gently without touching the capsule, 10 s. Note when a pop/hiss occurs.
3. Touch/wiggle the housing or connector without airflow, 10 s. Note contact pops; don't put the microphone directly against a strong air source or ears.
4. Direct gentle airflow over microphone (fan), **no speech**, 10 s.
5. Fan and normal speech simultaneously, 10 s. Crucial: should not lose intelligibility due to future gating.
6. Talk softly/loudly while still, then talk while moving mic. Compare the same sequences with `#define S10_ENABLED 0` (S11 stays enabled); this A/B comparison isolates AGC influence while preserving S8/S9.
7. If a pop occurs with `S10_ENABLED=0`, it is not generated by S10's gain algorithm; investigate mic/cable, ADC/front-end transients, earlier DSP, and DMA/codec separately.
8. Log whether pops correlate with `WBlocks` or `GStep10`; remember 1 sec summaries are not sample-aligned, so correlation isn't proof.

Acceptance for first stage: builds, no new `Late/Miss/DMA/Uskip` errors, small added CPU overhead, raw wind-band ratio rises under wind vs steady voice, and signals distinguish at least some wind from spoken voice. Do **not** merge the branch or implement suppression until real recorded data validates the detector and we understand hiss/popping.

## S12.1 diagnostic performance correction (following first hardware log)

First ~95 one-second reports showed `Proc` mostly 5.95–6.78 ms, maximum 6.86 ms, versus S11 maximum ~5.16 ms. All reported `Late/Miss/DMA/Uskip` remained 0. Measurement overhead was too high for production. Also `WRatio/WMax` often reached 100% while raw microphone RMS was only ~1–5 units: a very low-level IIR filter tail, not usable wind evidence.

S12.1 converts the diagnostic 480-sample accumulation from emulated `double` to hardware-supported `float` and sets reported ratio to 0 when raw RMS is below 25 PCM units. This **does not alter the signal path or the wind-candidate thresholds**, and remains an experimental detector. Rerun Performance100 and compare `Proc/Max` with the earlier 6.86 ms maximum. Inspect `WRMS`, `WRatio`, `WBlocks`, `GStep10` during the same conditions, and pay particular attention to artifacts when S10 gain is +10 to +18 dB. The first logs demonstrated up to +17.9 dB gain and occasional `AClip=5`, but the one-second summary cannot establish which event caused an audible pop. Measure any S10-enabled versus disabled difference with the same mic movement.

## S12.2 spectral baseline from labeled speech vs wind (still read-only)

The user provided three blowing-only bursts and then quiet/normal/loud speech. The old 20–200 Hz / total RMS ratio **overlaps**:
- Wind-only strong intervals: typically 22–49%.
- Speech intervals: roughly 5–64% (often 28–56%).
- The original 60% threshold misses most wind-only bursts; simply lowering it would misclassify many speech frames.

S12.2 adds **approximate** subbands from first-order lowpass differences at 20, 80, 200, and 1000 Hz: 20–80 Hz and 200–1000 Hz. These are not sharp FFT bins or calibrated wind classifiers. A 1-second summary of **active blocks** (raw microphone RMS >=250 PCM units) is added to avoid comparing the last silent block to a wind burst that occurred earlier in the second.

New fields:
- `WAct`: number of active 10ms raw microphone frames (threshold 250)
- `WSp`: those active frames for which post-S8/S9 VAD reports speech; **wind false positives count here**
- `WRawMax`: max raw RMS among active 10ms frames
- `WLo`: mean 20–80 Hz approximate-band RMS among active frames
- `WMid`: mean 200–1000 Hz approximate-band RMS among active frames
- `WRAvg`: arithmetic mean of 20–200 Hz / raw RMS ratio (%) among active frames
Existing `WRMS`/`WRatio` remain **last-block snapshots**, not one-second means.

No PCM change, no VAD/AGC change, no adaptive suppression and no new wind decision threshold. This is an engineering investigation and **must stay draft**.

**Test:** build and flash Performance100; log separated 10-second groups for quiet no speech, quiet voice, normal voice, loud voice, 3× wind-only bursts, and speech+wind. Label start/end or provide log sections. Watch CPU `Proc/Max` (pre-S12.2 max ~5.28ms) and `Late/Miss/DMA/Uskip` (must stay zero). Compare `WLo/WMid` and `WSp/WAct` across matched groups rather than accepting a class decision from the old ratio. Do not build suppressor logic until the features distinguish conditions, especially **speech+wind**.

## S12.3 conservative wind-suppression prototype — hardware test pending

**This is the first S12 version that changes audible PCM when enabled.** Original S7, S8, S9, S10 and S11 algorithms are unchanged; a separately bypassable S12 processing step is inserted **after S8 and before S9**, operating at 16k mono/160 samples per block. The raw wind feature detector runs before S7's HPF.

Build with `#define S12_SUPPRESSION 1` for experiment; set to `0` for exact S12.2 audio (suppression remains internally running as a detector/state comparison but output int16 PCM is copied unchanged). Retain `S12_DIAGNOSTICS=1` for this experiment.

**Prototype behavior and limitations:** A separate sustained wind criterion, raw mic RMS >=1100 PCM and approximate 20–80 Hz RMS >=170 PCM units, must hold 25 successive 10ms blocks (~250ms). Wind state stays active up to 45 blocks (~450ms) after criterion ceases. The signal is blended with a first-order ~220Hz high-pass at up to 60% mix, smoothed via `blend += .035*(target-blend)` per block; normal speech remains mixed in. This is **not** broadband noise cancellation, and strong wind energy in the 200–1000 Hz band will remain audible. Conditions are a conservative starting point, not calibrated classification. During a false positive, it may reduce low fundamentals of voices. No speech-muting, no all-band AGC attenuation, and no changes to existing S10 timing/coefficient parameters.

New UART fields appended: `WState` = number of 10ms blocks in sustained wind state per reporting interval; `WBlend` = most recent blend percentage [0, 60]; `SUP=a/b` = latest and interval-max suppression processing microseconds. Old `WBlocks` remains the original 60%-ratio experimental flag and is *not* the S12.3 decision. `WAct/WSp/WLo/WMid/WRAvg` remain.

Controlled validation:
1. Compile Performance100, flash Nucleo, verify zero `Late/Miss/DMA/Uskip`, log `Proc Max SUP`.
2. Listen to wind-only for 10–15s with `S12_SUPPRESSION=1`. Look for `WState>0`, `WBlend` rising and lower perceived wind rumble; do not expect broadband gusts to disappear.
3. Listen to quiet/normal/loud voice only with suppression on. `WState` should usually be zero and `WBlend=0`, with no altered vowels or pumping.
4. Compare same wind and voice tests with `S12_SUPPRESSION=0` (rebuild/flash). Because algorithm runs but copies PCM unchanged on bypass, this isolates audio effects rather than CPU effects.
5. Test speech *during* airflow; avoid losing intelligibility. If voice is affected noticeably, lower blend or increase sustained-wind threshold rather than masking the mic.
6. Monitor `AClip` and final `ClipD/F/NS/O` independently; limiter cannot recover upstream clipping.

**Do not merge PR #6 until board test and listening results are reviewed.** No hardware compile or flash performed by this GitHub workflow.
